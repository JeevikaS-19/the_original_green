The Scope 3 measurement gap

Most of an organisation's footprint is the part nobody measures.

01Fragmented data

Problem. Fleets sit in fuel logs, power in meter portals, cloud in provider bills, and people data in HR systems. None of them talk to each other.

Impact. Scope 1 and 2 are visible. Scope 3 is not.

Example. A campus tracks its buses (Scope 1) but has no method for staff commuting (Scope 3).

02Estimation black boxes

Problem. Tools that estimate Scope 3 rarely say how. There is no range and no visible assumption.

What organisations need. "This is estimated from headcount and a mode split. Confidence ±30%. Here is why."

03Category complexity

Problem. Cloud, hardware, commute and waste each need a different method.

Our approach. Activity-based where the data exists (instance-hours, device counts, km). Spend-based only as a fallback, and labelled as such.

04Hardware invisibility

Problem. Device lifecycle emissions are rarely tracked. An ERP sees "IT spend", not "500 laptops over a five-year life".

Our approach. An asset register, with embodied carbon spread over each device's lifespan.

From public reports. Infosys reports that its FY 2025-26 laptop procurement, tied to product carbon footprints, avoided 8,955 tCO₂e. TCS's commuting category alone is 302,962 tCO₂e of its FY 2025-26 Scope 3 (573,872 tCO₂e in total).

The innovation

Three layers of transparent calculation.

Layer 1Input Trackers parsed. Gaps proposed and labelled.
Layer 2Calculate Each scope, its factor, its range.
Layer 3Reduce Actions ranked by tonnes per rupee.

Architecture

Each layer is documented, auditable and extensible.

Layer 1 · Input

  • Trackers and files
  • Parse and categorise
  • Propose assumptions
→

Layer 2 · Calculate

  • Scope 1: litres × fuel factor
  • Scope 2: kWh × grid factor
  • Scope 3: activity, or spend as fallback
  • Confidence propagation
→

Layer 3 · Reduce

  • Rank actions by cost per tonne
  • Scenarios: stack actions
  • Reduction path

Each layer in detail

The working, shown.

Each upload is tagged measured. Anything we fill in is tagged estimated and says why. Each estimate lowers the confidence one level.

What the uploads give

  • Fleet log: 2 buses, 85,000 km each a year
  • Meter export: 12 monthly readings
  • Waste log: monthly kg by category

What we propose

  • Commute split from a benchmark, if there is no survey (shown as estimated)
  • Hardware count from headcount, if there is no register (illustrative)
