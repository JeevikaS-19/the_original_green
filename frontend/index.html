The Scope 3 measurement gap

Most of an organisation's footprint is the part nobody measures.

Four reasons the number on your dashboard is incomplete. Select one to see what we do about it.

+

Fragmented data

Fleets, power and cloud each live in a different tool.

The problem. Fleet logs, meter portals, cloud bills and HR data never meet. Scope 1 and 2 are visible. Scope 3 is not.

A campus tracks its buses, but has no method for staff commuting.

+

Estimation black boxes

Tools estimate, but never say how.

The problem. No range, no visible assumption. A number with hidden error looks more certain than it is.

"Estimated from headcount and a mode split. Confidence ±30%. Here is why."

+

Category complexity

Cloud, hardware, commute and waste each need their own method.

Our approach. Activity-based where the data exists: instance hours, device counts, kilometres. Spend-based only as a labelled fallback.

+

Hardware invisibility

An ERP sees IT spend, not devices and their lifespans.

Our approach. An asset register, with embodied carbon spread over each device's life. Factors are marked illustrative until a verified source is added.

Public reports show the scale: TCS's FY 2025-26 Scope 3 is 573,872 tCO₂e, and its commuting category alone is 302,962 tCO₂e.

How it works

Three layers, from file to action.

Each layer does one job, and passes on what it knows and how sure it is.

01

Layer 1

Input

Trackers are read as exports. Gaps are proposed, and each proposal is labelled.

02

Layer 2

Calculate

Every scope gets its factor, its formula and its range, and nothing is double counted.

03

Layer 3

Reduce

Actions are ranked by tonnes saved per lakh of rupees, with payback, and can be stacked.

Architecture

Each layer is documented, auditable and extensible.

Layer 1

Input

  • Tracker exports and uploads
  • Parsing and categorisation
  • Proposed assumptions, labelled

Layer 2

Calculate

  • Scope 1: litres × fuel factor
  • Scope 2: kWh × grid factor
  • Scope 3: activity, spend as fallback
  • Confidence propagation

Layer 3

Reduce

  • Actions ranked by cost per tonne
  • Scenarios that stack
  • A reduction path over time
