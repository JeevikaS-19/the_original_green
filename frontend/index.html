Cross-scope carbon reporting

Your footprint, from the trackers you already use.

We don't ask you to track anything again. We read the exports your fleet, energy, cloud and HR tools already produce, calculate Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, and show the actions that save the most per rupee.

Open the demo See how it works

Demo values are illustrative and built from the team's emission factors.

What we read

  • Vehicle logs — fuel type and annual km
  • Meter exports — monthly kWh and state
  • Cloud bills — instance hours and cost
  • Asset registers — devices and lifespans
  • Commute surveys — mode split and distance
  • Waste logs — solid, e-waste, wastewater

Three kinds of user, one report

Pick the scale that matches you. Each one needs a different amount of data, and the report adjusts to match.

Individual

A personal footprint

You give us: your car's fuel log and your electricity bills.

You get: a grade, your biggest drivers, and a few changes that help.

Small scale

School or small company

You give us: your vehicle log, DG set, meter export, asset register, commute survey and waste logs.

You get: a Scope 1, 2 and 3 report with confidence ranges and a ranked action plan.

Large scale

Large enterprise

You give us: fleet telematics, multi-site meters, IT asset registers and HR commute data.

You get: a BRSR-ready report with grade against benchmarks and ROI-ranked actions.

Two ways to share your data

The more detail you share, the tighter the range. If you can't share detail, we still work from what you have.

Open-source data

Full exports from your trackers

Detailed vehicle, meter, asset, commute and waste data gives a tight result.

Confidence about ±15%

Closed-source data

Budget totals only

Fuel spend, the electricity bill and headcount are enough to get a rough estimate. We say how rough.

Confidence about ±40%

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect trackers

    We read the metadata your existing tools export. Nothing is retyped.

  2. 2

    Validate and route

    Every file is checked. Missing values are estimated and flagged, not hidden.

  3. 3

    Calculate

    Scope 1 fuel, Scope 2 electricity, and Scope 3 devices, commute and waste.

  4. 4

    Act

    Reduction actions ranked by tons saved per lakh of rupees, with payback periods.
