How it works

From upload to a grade in four steps.

Scroll to follow the data through the platform.

Step 1Trackers are read as exports, not re-typed.
Step 1 · Connect

Read the trackers you already use

Fleet logs, meter exports, cloud bills, asset registers and commute surveys. We read metadata, not screens.

Step 2 · Validate

Check every file, flag every gap

Missing fields are estimated with a stated reason. Anything estimated is marked, and the range widens to match.

Step 3 · Calculate

Scope by scope, with a range

Fuel, electricity, devices, commute and waste, each with its factor and its source status.

Step 4 · Act

Ranked actions and scenarios

Each action shows the tonnes it saves, its cost, its payback, and the grade it would earn.
