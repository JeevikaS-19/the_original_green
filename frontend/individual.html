Set up in four steps

  1. Choose an activityPick one from the list: car, electricity, commute, laptop, waste.
  2. Enter the amountThe unit is shown next to the box. Use the number from your own record.
  3. Press Add activityIt is saved in this browser. Edit or delete it any time.
  4. Check your footprintThe total, grade and scope breakdown update straight away.
