A monthly meter export. The platform reads the twelve readings, averages them, annualises them, and calculates Scope 2.

How the platform reads it

STEP 1

Read the export

Twelve monthly rows, with the region in each row.

STEP 2

Map the columns

The kWh column is matched by name. Alternative names such as kwh_consumed are accepted.

STEP 3

Validate

Each reading must be a number. A missing month would reduce the sample and be flagged.

STEP 4

Calculate Scope 2

Annual kWh × 0.757 kg CO₂e per kWh (CEA combined margin, FY 2023-24).
