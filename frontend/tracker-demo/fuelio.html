Fleet tracker

Fuel and fleet log

A fuel-log export, one row per vehicle. The platform reads the vehicle, its fuel and its distance, then calculates Scope 1 for the fleet.

Demo. This shows how the platform reads a fuel-log export. It is not a connection to any product.

Sample export: fleet log (CSV)

vehicle_id,make,model,year,fuel_type,annual_km,fuel_efficiency_l_per_100km
BUS_01,Bus,Diesel 40-seater,2017,diesel,85000,30
BUS_02,Bus,Diesel 40-seater,2019,diesel,70000,29
VAN_01,Van,Diesel minivan,2020,diesel,25000,11

How the platform reads it

STEP 1

Read the export

A CSV with one row per vehicle is read as it is. Nothing is typed in.

STEP 2

Map the columns

Headers are matched by name, including common aliases such as annual_km or distance_km.

STEP 3

Validate each row

Make and fuel type are required. A missing distance would be estimated and marked.

STEP 4

Calculate Scope 1

Litres = distance × efficiency ÷ 100, then litres × 2.68 kg CO₂e per litre (diesel).
